How these are matched

Population comes from Census ACS estimates for Portland School District 1J (calendar year). Enrollment is PPS October counts, so the 2016-17 school year is plotted at 2016. Elementary, middle and high population groups are estimated by splitting the Census 10-14 band evenly, so they are approximate. Pre-K enrollment covers only ages 3-4 against a 0-4 population, so treat that pair as a rough comparison. ◆ marks 2020, which is an ACS 5-year estimate. PPS enrollment also differs from population because of private school, home school, charters and transfers.
